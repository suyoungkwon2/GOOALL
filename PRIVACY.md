# GOOALL Privacy Policy

*Last updated: Oct 25, 2026 · Su Young*

## Summary

**GOOALL keeps your data on your iPhone. We do not collect, store or share it.**

- The app reads your step count (and exercise minutes, if you choose that goal) from Apple Health. It only reads; it never writes.
- Everything is processed and stored on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server.
- There is no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
- Health data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or data mining.
- If you buy a theme, Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account information.
- Deleting the app deletes the data it stored.

## Information the app accesses

The app accesses three kinds of information, and all of them stay on your device.

- **From Apple Health, with your permission:** your step count and, if you choose an exercise goal, your exercise minutes for the current day or week. The app only reads this data and never writes to Apple Health.
- **What you enter in the app:** your goal (steps or minutes, daily or weekly), your chosen motivation theme, and your setup progress.
- **Your purchases:** which themes you have unlocked. The app checks this with Apple on your device (see [In-app purchases](#in-app-purchases)).

We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any account details.

## How we use it

The app uses this information only to show your progress. It compares your steps or exercise minutes with your goal, then picks the image that matches your progress for the app and its widgets. Purchase information is used only to unlock the themes you bought.

We do not use Apple Health data for advertising, marketing, profiling or data mining, and we use it for nothing beyond the features described here.

## In-app purchases

Some themes may be offered as a one-time in-app purchase. Each purchase unlocks one theme. There is no subscription.

- **Payment is handled by Apple.** Purchases go through the App Store and your Apple Account, under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We never receive your name, email address, Apple Account or payment card details.
- **Checking your purchases happens on your device.** The app uses Apple's StoreKit to confirm which themes you own. We do not run a server to check or store purchases, and we keep no purchase history about you.
- **What we can see:** Apple gives developers sales and payment reports (for example, how many copies of a theme were sold in a country). These reports do not identify you.
- **Restoring purchases:** themes you bought are tied to your Apple Account. On a new device or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account and use "Restore Purchases" in the app's settings (available once paid themes are offered).
- **Refunds:** refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Storage, sharing and tracking

Your data stays on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. The app keeps your goal, theme and latest progress in a storage area that only the app and its widgets can use on your iPhone (an iOS App Group). Your purchase record is kept by Apple under your Apple Account, not by us.

- We run no servers and keep no copy of your data.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not sell, rent or share your information.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the backup may include the app's stored settings. Apple manages those backups under your Apple account, and we cannot access them.

## Widgets and your device

Widgets show your progress image and numbers on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, so anyone who can see your screen can see them. Lock Screen widgets can be visible while your iPhone is locked. You can remove a widget at any time, or change what appears on your Lock Screen in iOS Settings.

## Your choices and controls

- **Health access:** you can change or withdraw the app's access to Apple Health at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings (menu names vary by iOS version). Without access, the app cannot update your progress.
- **Settings:** you can change your goal or theme in the app whenever you like.
- **Deleting data:** deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Your purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be restored later.

The developer does not collect or hold your personal data, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete. If you live in the EU, the UK or California, your rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA still apply. Contact us (see the last section) and we will answer within the time the law requires.

## Children's privacy

GOOALL is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the app sends no data to us, we hold no information about children. If you believe a child has contacted us, write to us and we will delete the message. Purchases made by children are subject to Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy settings.

## Apple and other third parties

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store and may be tested through TestFlight, both under Apple's own privacy policy. In-app purchases are processed by Apple as described above. If you turn on "Share With App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may give us crash and usage reports that do not identify you. We use no other third-party services.

## Health information notice

GOOALL is a motivational tool with playful visuals. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it promises no physical result. Talk to a qualified professional before changing your exercise or health routine.
