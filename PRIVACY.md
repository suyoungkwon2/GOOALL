# GOOALL Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

GOOALL ("the app") turns your step count into a picture on your home screen widget. This policy explains what data the app uses and what happens to it.

## The short version
- The app reads your **step count** from Apple Health, with your permission.
- Everything stays **on your iPhone**. The app has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.
- We don't collect, sell, or share any of your data.

## What the app reads
- **Step count (Apple Health, read-only).** Used only to calculate progress toward the goal you set and to choose which picture your widget shows. The app never writes to Apple Health and does not read any other health data.

## What the app stores
The following is saved only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widget:
- Your goal (daily or weekly, target number of steps) and chosen theme
- Your widget background color
- The latest step total and progress, so the widget can show it
- Whether you finished onboarding

None of this leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Health data
Health data obtained through Apple HealthKit is not used for advertising or data mining, is not shared with third parties, and is not stored in iCloud or on any server by the app.

## Your choices
- You can turn Health access on or off anytime in **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → GOOALL**. The app keeps working without it (it just shows 0 steps).
- You can delete all app data by deleting the app.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
